Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and offered by Apps-Tools (including USA Weather & Radar, weather forecast, live radar, and related mobile applications published under the Apps-Tools developer account). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our apps.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Location Data (Approximate and Precise): The app requests access to your device's location solely to provide accurate local weather forecasts, hourly temperatures, and radar map visualization for your current area. Location data is processed on-device and transmitted anonymously to weather API providers to retrieve relevant weather data. We do not track, store, or sell your continuous location history.
  • Device and App Information: We may access non-personally identifiable technical details such as OS version, device model, and language preferences to deliver the appropriate UI language and optimize performance.

2. Third-Party Services

The app relies on trusted third-party providers to deliver services:

  • Weather Data Providers (Open-Meteo / RainViewer): Anonymous geographic coordinates are sent to retrieve weather and radar tiles.
  • Google Play Services & Google Maps: Used for map rendering and core app functionalities.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Third-party ad networks may use advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and non-personalized diagnostic cookies/signals to display relevant advertisements in compliance with Google Play Policies.

3. Data Retention and Security

The app does not require account creation or registration. User preferences (such as selected favorite cities, temperature unit °C/°F, and theme mode) are stored locally on your device using encrypted shared preferences and can be cleared anytime by uninstalling or clearing the app's cache.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
